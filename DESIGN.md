# Product launch direction

Preserve the existing 17hands brand: plum canvas, rose gold accents, amethyst actions, and the established display and body fonts. Digital Defenders uses an editorial workshop layout with generous spacing, a numbered curriculum, and a practical checklist. Avoid hacker imagery, fake metrics, fear-led copy, and extra visual effects. Natural is an upcoming product area, without invented services or availability.

Launch scope: product navigation, two dedicated routes, workshop inquiries through the existing booking and email channels, and a browser-local family checklist. No new data collection or dependencies.
